Suggested Teaching Guidelines for 
Natural Language Processing & Computer Vision 
PGCP-AI August 2026 
 
 
 
Duration: 120 Hours: 40 Theory (T) + 60 Lab (L) + 20 Self-Learning (SL) 
 
Objective: To Introduce the students to Natural Language Processing &Computer Vision. 
 
Prerequisites: Good knowledge of Python Programming and fundamentals of Artificial Intelligence. 
 
Evaluation method: Theory exam – 40%, Lab exam – 40%, Internal Assessment– 20%  
List of Course material  
Text Book: 
Natural Language Processing, by Pushpak Bhattacharyya, Aditya Joshi / Wiley India, 2023 
 
Reference Book: 
1. The Big Book of NLP Techniques by Shlomo Vaknin 
2. Theory and Practice of NLP Coaching: A Psychological Approach by Bruce Grimley 
 
Note: 
• Lab assignments are indicative; faculty shall provide adequate assignments for 
better practice.
